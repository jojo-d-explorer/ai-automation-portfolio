About the team
The Risk Program Management Office is a unique role that sits at the intersection of Operations, Go To Market (GTM), and Risk Compliance teams. Our mission is to enable users to seamlessly meet evolving compliance standards in a complex regulatory environment by bringing the right set of cross-functional stakeholders to enable their success. As an enablement function, we work with many teams including Operations, Product, Eng, GTM, and others.
We are expanding our scope into one of Stripe's most consequential strategic priorities and launching in new markets. This work is new, fast-moving, and high-ambiguity — and we're looking for someone who thrives in exactly that environment. If you enjoy working on high-impact projects on a global scale, building programs from the ground up, and operating at the intersection of compliance, strategy, and execution — you'll love this role.
What you’ll do
New Markets Program Management
Own end-to-end program management for new markets across Stripe's roadmap, focusing on Americas and EMEA region
Drive operational readiness across workstreams, tracking milestones, surfacing risks, and unblocking dependencies
Navigate high ambiguity — this is a 0→1 space where you will be expected to figure out the right approach, not wait for one to be handed to you
Build repeatable frameworks, playbooks, and reporting standards to support a growing and complex portfolio of markets
Cross-Functional Leadership
Represent our team in senior level conversations across Stripe and have high judgment and accountability
Provide critical coordination across Risk teams (Ops, Product, Eng, Strategy, and others) to ensure alignment and execution
Partner closely with stakeholders to maintain momentum across a fast-moving roadmap
Facilitate alignment across teams with competing priorities, resolve ambiguity, and ensure clear ownership at every stage of execution
Operations
Identify operational impact, gap assessment, and other operational considerations to launch in new markets
Ensure operational readiness across complex portfolio of programs, including forecasting, monitoring, and last-mile execution
Maintain high operational rigor and execution discipline
Who you are

Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to write sharp 1-pagers and present credibly to senior audiences

Experience working across global teams and time zones, including flexibility to join calls outside standard US business hours as needed
Preferred qualifications
Experience working on international expansion, market entry, regulatory, or licensing programs

East Coast or Central Time candidates are preferred, or candidates willing to flex EMEA and APAC timezones
This role is available either in an office or a remote location (35+ miles or 56+ km from a Stripe office).
Office-assigned Stripes spend at least 50% of the time in a given month in their local office or with users. This hits a balance between bringing people together for in-person collaboration and learning from each other, while supporting flexibility about how to do this in a way that makes sense for individuals and their teams.
A remote location is defined as being 35 miles (56 kilometers) or more from one of our offices. While you would be welcome to come into the office for team/business meetings, on-sites, meet-ups, and events, our expectation is you would regularly work from home rather than a Stripe office. Stripe does not cover the cost of relocating to a remote location. We encourage you to apply for roles that match the location where you currently live or plan to live.
The annual US base salary range for this role is $127,600 - $191,400. For sales roles, the range provided is the role’s On Target Earnings ("OTE") range, meaning that the range includes both the sales commissions/sales bonuses target and annual base salary for the role. This salary range may be inclusive of several career levels at Stripe and will be narrowed during the interview process based on a number of factors, including the candidate’s experience, qualifications, and location. Applicants interested in this role and who are not located in the US may request the annual salary range for their location during the interview process.
Additional benefits for this role may include: equity, company bonus or sales commissions/bonuses; 401(k) plan; medical, dental, and vision benefits; and wellness stipends.
